### Why away mode matters
`computeInboundAiActive = (awayMode || generateOnly) && defaultAiActive` (`src/lib/generate-only.ts`) and
`shouldAutoSendReply = aiActive && (awayMode || autoSendOverride)` (`src/lib/webhook-processor.ts:727`).
With generate-only OFF and away mode OFF, new Instagram leads get `aiActive=false` and nothing auto-sends: **silence, not go-live**.
So the PM flip is two settings, in this order:

1. `awayModeInstagram = true` (generate-only still on → still nothing delivers; the away-mode rescue sweep re-fires `scheduleAIReply` for leads who wrote in the last 30 min and are waiting, but under generate-only those land as suggestions).
2. `generateOnlyInstagram = false` → live.

Doing it the other way round creates leads that are permanently human-only.
