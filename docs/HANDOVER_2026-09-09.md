## 1. What this product is

**Convlo** (formerly QualifyDMs, repo `ai-dm-setter`, prod `https://qualifydms.io`,
Vercel team `dm-setter-team-developers`, project `ai-dm-setter`) is an AI DM
lead-qualifier for course sellers. A lead DMs a client's Instagram or Facebook
page; Meta delivers a webhook; the engine resolves the workspace, creates a
Lead + Conversation, generates a reply from the client's uploaded **script**
(steps → branches → actions) and **persona**, runs it through quality and
egress gates, and delivers it back through Meta. Operators watch a dashboard,
can take over, and get notifications on holds.

**Client-two blocker (the thing everything is aimed at):** the engine works on
the first client's script (daetradez) because a month of hand-debugging found
every disagreement between script and engine. Client two arrives with a script
nobody has adversarially tested, and is Instagram-first. "Works on ANY script"
and "Instagram at Facebook parity" are the two conditions for the next client.

Key architecture facts that keep biting (all verified in code):

- **Branch selection** is an LLM judge + token scorer (`ai-engine.ts` ~3184–3300) that returns NULL on low confidence; NULL empties the required-message set and makes the gate blind. Single-branch steps are now selected deterministically; multi-branch is the M5 compiler's job.
- **Step advancement** is `computeSystemStage` in `script-state-recovery.ts` rescanning history every turn with daetradez-hardcoded detectors in `script-step-progression.ts` (`MANDATORY_ASK_STEPS`, `CALL_PROPOSAL_PREREQS`, …). This is what breaks on a new script. The M5 compiler replaces it.
- **Egress gate** `src/lib/state-machine/{types,can-send,shadow}.ts` sits at the physical Meta send (`instagram.ts sendDM`, `facebook.ts sendMessage`). Authoritative on Facebook only (`FIX_D_CANSEND_AUTHORITATIVE=FACEBOOK`), shadow on Instagram. It logs `EgressShadowLog` rows; `agreed=false` rows are the review queue.
- **Reply delivery** has two paths: inline `after()` in the webhook route when the reply delay ≤ 45s (inside `maxDuration=120`), else the per-minute cron `process-scheduled-replies`. See §5 for why both died once.
- **Prompt assembly** is ~40 template replaces + 14 appends + 59 retry-loop overrides with no precedence — M5 item 6.
